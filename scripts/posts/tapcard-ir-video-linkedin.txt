Your announcement is out. Did your shareholders see it?

An email can sit unread in a promotions tab, be caught by a spam filter, or never reach someone who is not on your list.

With TapCard for investor relations, the people who follow your company get a notification on their phone when you announce: a one-line headline with a link to the announcement on your own website. It is a message they asked for, and it goes straight to their phone, not their inbox.

And you can see how well it worked:

• how many followers each headline was sent to
• how many tapped through to the announcement
• what time of day they read it
• how many started following around the time you posted

People join by scanning a QR code on your results slide, annual report or AGM notice, or one for each event or roadshow slot. They follow, and choose whether to share their name and email. You see only the people who choose to.

TapCard does not provide RNS: the announcement and the data stay on your own website, and you post only once it is public.

The 30-second video shows it with an example company. It is early days and some parts are still being built.

For IR and PR teams, and the agencies behind them: how do you measure whether your announcements are actually read?
