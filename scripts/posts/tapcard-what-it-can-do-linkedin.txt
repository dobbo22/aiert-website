Most local businesses don't have a marketing problem. They have a "nobody can tell I have something on" problem.

We have just published a plain guide to what TapCard can do for a café, restaurant or shop. It comes down to two jobs:

1. Grow your followers. Put your QR code where customers already are: the door, the till, the tables, the receipt. Anyone who scans it can see and save your card. People who follow you in the TapCard app get your offers and news.

2. Post an offer or some news. A line, a deadline and a staff PIN, and everyone following your card gets a notification. No algorithm deciding who sees it, and never more than two notifications a week from any one business, so people don't tune you out.

Then you can see what worked: how many follow you, how many claimed an offer, how many came in and used it.
